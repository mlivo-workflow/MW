PED 2014/68/EU — Equipment Category Classifier

Determine the pressure equipment category (I–IV or Article 4(3)) based on Annex II, Tables 1–8 of Directive 2014/68/EU. Free to use — no login required.

Fluid Group 1 Dangerous: flammable, explosive, oxidising, or acutely toxic — including liquefied gases.
Fluid Group 2 All other fluids not classified Group 1 (e.g. water, air, nitrogen, non-toxic refrigerants).
PS — max. allowable pressure Gauge pressure in bar. Equipment with PS ≤ 0.5 bar is out of PED scope (Art. 1.6).
V — volume (vessels) Internal volume in litres. For piping, use nominal size DN (mm) instead.

Category overview

Art. 4(3) — SEP

Sound Engineering Practice. No CE marking required, but equipment must still be safe.

Category I

Module A — Internal production control. Manufacturer's self-declaration.

Category II

Module A2, D1, or E1. Notified Body surveillance or assessment.

Category III

Modules B+D, B+F, B+E, B+C2, H, or H1. Full Notified Body involvement.

Category IV

Modules B+D, B+F, G, or H1. Highest level of conformity assessment.

Important disclaimer: This tool provides indicative guidance only, based on the graphical Annex II charts of Directive 2014/68/EU. Boundary values are numerical approximations of the graphical curves. This tool does not replace a formal conformity assessment, professional engineering judgement, or consultation with a Notified Body. Always verify classification against the official directive text.
