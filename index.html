What we do

Engineering teams lose time and money due to unclear workflows, inconsistent documentation, and poor data handovers. Mlivo Workflow helps organizations standardize documentation and data flows, reduce rework, and create repeatable, scalable workflows across teams and projects.

Standardize

Folder structures, naming, revisions, and handover rules that actually stick.

Optimize

Remove duplication, manual steps, and coordination gaps between departments.

Enable

Coaching so teams apply standards consistently without constant supervision.

To ensure clarity and focus, the following services are explicitly excluded:

  • Engineering design or layout
  • Piping or plant design
  • Calculations or stress analysis
  • Code/standard compliance assessments
  • Technical approvals or sign-off
  • Responsibility for technical content

Responsibility for technical content remains entirely with the client.
